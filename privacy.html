Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and explain here how we collect, use, share, and protect your information when you interact with SunNest.

Last updated: December 10, 2025

1. Information We Collect

  • Account and contact details: name, email, phone, shipping/billing addresses.
  • Order and payment details (processed via secure payment providers; we do not store full card data).
  • Usage data: pages viewed, device/browser info, IP address, and interaction logs.
  • Support communications and feedback you provide.

2. How We Use Information

  • Process and deliver orders, provide customer support, and manage your account.
  • Improve site performance, product experience, and security.
  • Send transactional messages; send marketing only with your consent and allow opt-out anytime.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. Legal Bases (where applicable)

We rely on performance of a contract (fulfilling orders), legitimate interests (site security, improvements), consent (marketing cookies/emails), and legal compliance.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies for site functionality and may use analytics/performance cookies to understand usage. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; disabling some may affect site features.

5. Sharing Your Information

  • Service providers: payments, shipping partners, analytics, and hosting, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal reasons: to comply with law, respond to requests, or protect rights and safety.
  • Business transfers: in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, data may transfer with appropriate protections.

6. Data Security

We apply industry-standard safeguards, encryption in transit, and access controls. No method is 100% secure; please use strong passwords and notify us of suspected issues.

7. Data Retention

We retain data for as long as needed to provide services, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Data no longer needed is securely deleted or anonymized.

8. Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete certain personal data, subject to legal limits.
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.
  • Control cookies via browser or device settings.
  • Request data portability or restriction where applicable by law.

9. Children's Privacy

SunNest is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child provided data, contact us to remove it.

10. International Users

If you access our services from outside India, you consent to processing in India and any other countries where our service providers operate, subject to appropriate safeguards.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will revise the "Last updated" date and post changes here. Significant changes may be notified via email or site notice.

12. Contact

For questions or requests, email privacy@sunnest.example or call +91-00000-00000.
