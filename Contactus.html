Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do solar heated blankets work?

Our blankets use advanced solar panels to convert sunlight into energy, which is stored in a built-in battery. The heating elements are activated when you need warmth, providing up to 12 hours of continuous heat.

What is your shipping policy?

We offer free shipping across India for all orders. Delivery typically takes 3-7 business days depending on your location. International shipping is available with rates calculated at checkout.

Do you offer warranty on your products?

Yes! All SunNest products come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. Extended warranty options are available at purchase.

Can I return a product if I'm not satisfied?

We offer a 30-day return policy. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, you can return it for a full refund or exchange within 30 days of delivery.

How do I care for my solar heated products?

Remove the battery pack and solar panel before washing. Machine wash on gentle cycle with cold water and air dry. Detailed care instructions are included with each product.
