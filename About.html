About SunNest

Pioneering Sustainable Warmth Through Solar Innovation

Our Story

Founded in 2020, SunNest was born from a simple yet powerful idea: to harness the abundant energy of the sun to provide warmth and comfort wherever you are. Our journey began when our founder, an avid mountaineer, experienced the challenges of staying warm during remote expeditions.

Combining expertise in renewable energy and textile engineering, we developed our first solar-heated blanket prototype. The overwhelming positive response from the outdoor community inspired us to expand our product line and make sustainable heating accessible to everyone.

Today, SunNest stands at the forefront of solar heating technology, serving over 50,000 customers worldwide and continuously innovating to create products that are both environmentally responsible and exceptionally functional.

Our Mission

To revolutionize personal heating solutions by making solar-powered warmth accessible, affordable, and sustainable for everyone. We're committed to reducing carbon footprints while enhancing comfort and convenience in daily life and outdoor adventures.

Our Vision

To become the global leader in solar heating technology, creating a world where sustainable warmth is the norm rather than the exception. We envision a future where every individual can experience comfort without compromising the health of our planet.
